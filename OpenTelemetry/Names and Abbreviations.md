|Term|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**OTel**|Short for OpenTelemetry. Written "OTel", not "OTEL"|
|**OTLP**|The OpenTelemetry Protocol|
|**OTelCol**|Short for OpenTelemetry Collector|
|**OpenTracing (OT)** and **OpenCensus (OC)**|The two projects that merged to form OpenTelemetry|
|**Contrib**|Optional components, such as vendor exporters, kept in separate "contrib" repositories|
|**Distribution**|A customised version of an upstream OpenTelemetry repository|

## Course at a glance

The course has six modules. Each one ends with a short quiz.

1. **Module 1:** Understanding Systems & Dependencies
2. **Module 2:** Incident Management Fundamentals
3. **Module 3:** Verticals of Monitoring
4. **Module 4:** Structured Investigation Process
5. **Module 5:** Basic Risk Awareness
6. **Module 6:** Basic Communication


## What You Will Learn

- **System Awareness:** How to deconstruct a complex application into its component layers—from the UI the user touches to the infrastructure running in the cloud.
- **The Five Verticals of Monitoring:** How to move beyond "is it up?" to a 360-degree view of System Health, Application Performance, Functionality, and Databases.
- **Incident Framework:** The industry-standard definitions of Incidents, Problems, and Changes, and the metrics (MTTR, MTTD, MTTA, etc.) we use to measure our success.
- **The Art of Investigation**: A step-by-step methodology to move from an alert to a resolution without panicking or guessing.
- **Risk and Communication:** How to evaluate the danger of a situation and how to speak to stakeholders and teammates in the middle of a "fire."
